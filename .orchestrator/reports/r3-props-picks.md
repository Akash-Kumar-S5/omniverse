   - Retain `gothic_statue.glb` on its plinth.
   - Scatter `candles-altar.glb` and `skull-bones.glb` on the niche pedestal for ancient sanctuary depth.

3. **Chapter 2 (Work / Colonnade):**
   - Replace the ember helix swarm with physical props on stands or stone plinths along the colonnade:
     - Role 1 (Engineer): `greatsword.glb` (standing blade)
     - Role 2 (Associate Engineer): `shield-heater.glb` (propped against pillar)
     - Role 3 (Intern): `helmet-greathelm.glb` (resting on plinth)
     - Role 4 (Intern, full-stack): `battleaxe.glb` or `sword-knight.glb`
   - Flank colonnade pillars with `torch-wall.glb` and `banner.glb`.

4. **Chapter 3 (Projects / Gallery):**
   - Retain the classical framed oil paintings in `standing_picture_frame_01.glb`.
   - Drop the background ember swarm glyph.
   - Illumination from single warm fire/torch beam hitting the flanking stone column.

5. **Chapter 4 (Contact / Central Hearth):**
   - Central hearth fire pit with real flame (`fire-flame-3d.glb` and/or `flame-spritesheet-6x1.png`) and `hearth-logs.glb` embers.
